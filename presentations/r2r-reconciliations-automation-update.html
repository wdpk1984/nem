Section 01
Strategic Objectives

From manual risk to standard control

Today's risk
Program objective
Manual, fragmented preparation
Data pulled by hand from BlackLine and SAP
→
Automated, rules-based preparation
Bot extracts BlackLine and SAP data and builds each template
No common standard
Varying formats limit consistency and audit reliance
→
One standard template per account type
Predefined business rules applied the same way every month
Effort on low-risk work
Preparer time absorbed before analysis even begins
→
Focus on true exceptions
Preparers start directly at analysis and investigation
Source: Robotics Project Charter, RTR Reconciliations (Business Problem); PDD-R2R Reconciliation-AP v1.1 (Objectives)
Section 02
Operational Improvement

One bot, four steps, every close

01
Trigger & extract
BlackLine readiness email starts the run; monthly reports are downloaded
02
Scope & filter
Agreed account scope and filtering logic applied automatically
03
Build templates
Standard template per account, filled with SAP data and screenshots
04
Publish & notify
Outputs saved to the global SharePoint site; preparers notified
Human judgement stays where it matters: preparers analyse differences and upload to BlackLine; approvers review and certify, kept outside bot scope by design.
Sources: AP & AR bot deployment announcement (15 Sep 2026); Robotics Project Charter, RTR Reconciliations (Scope)
